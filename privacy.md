# Keeprs Privacy Policy

*Last updated 27 October 2026*

Keeprs copies photos from a Ricoh GR IV camera to your iPhone. That is all it does, and this page describes everything it touches.

## What the app does with your data

- **Photos.** The app asks for *add-only* access to your photo library. It saves the photos you import and nothing else; it cannot read, list or modify the photos already on your phone.
- **The camera.** The app talks to your camera over Bluetooth and the camera's own Wi-Fi network. The camera's Wi-Fi name and password are stored in your iPhone's Keychain so you only pair once. They never leave the device.
- **Import history and keepers.** A record of which photos have been imported, and which you starred as keepers, is kept on your iPhone so the app knows what is new. It is stored only on the device.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (JPEG size, filters, stacking) are stored on the device.
- **Diagnostics.** The app keeps a short log of connection and import events on the device, which you can read under Settings › Diagnostics and share yourself if you choose to. The app never sends it anywhere on its own.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No servers: the app makes no network requests except to your camera on its local network.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
- No data is collected by the developer. There is nothing to request, correct or delete, because nothing is held off your device.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored. Photos you imported stay in your photo library, as they are yours.
